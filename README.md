hello guys this is my game nested worlds
my name is aahil so i made this in godot engine

WHAT IS THIS GAME
you play as kael and the universe is collapsing
you have to save the archive hub world by jumping inside magical orbs
each orb is a different world
the hub world in which you spawn has a door and a secret orb to open the door you have to step on two different pressure plates one in red world and one in blue world
red world has a heavy bridge and lever puzzle
blue world is like inception you go deep inside a world inside a world
green world has a secret pressure plate puzzle you must step sun then eye then moon then star
finally you get the golden void world orb
you then go in the void world and win the game
this puts the world inside itself and makes a recursive paradox to win

CONTROLS TO PLAY
w a s d keys to run around
space bar to jump
walk up to an orb to grab it
walk up to an empty socket to put the orb down
mouse to look around

COOL FEATURES
i added photoreal hdri sky and crazy lighting
custom sound effects for walking picking up orbs and portal diving

pls play and enjoy the game thank you
