WHAT IS THIS GAME ABOUT
so what is this game let me tell you everything
you play as a guy named kael
the universe is collapsing very bad and everything is breaking around you
you have to save the archive hub world
how do you save it you ask
you have to jump inside very magical orbs
each orb is not just a small ball it is a full different world hiding inside

THE WORLDS AND PUZZLES
the hub world is the main place in which you spawn first
in this hub world there is a very transparent wall and behind the wall is a secret orb hiding
to open this big door you have to do hard work
you have to find and step on two different pressure plates to open it
and turn on a lever in the micro blue world
so where are the plates
one plate is hidden far away in the red world
the red world has a bridge
the second plate is in the blue world
the blue world is crazy it is exactly like inception movie
you go deep inside a world inside another world to find the second plate it is very trippy
then there is also the green world
the green world has a very secret pressure plate puzzle
you must step on the stone plates in exact order
first step on the sun plate then the eye plate then the moon plate then the star plate
if you do this right you finally get the golden void world orb

HOW TO WIN
after you get the golden void world orb you go in the void world 
when you do this you finally win the game
this puts the entire world inside itself and makes a big recursive paradox to win it is very cool

CONTROLS TO PLAY
w a s d keys to run around the map
space bar to jump over things
shift key is very important you use shift key to skip the story
also you use the same shift key to pick up a orb and also drop a orb
mouse to look around everywhere
